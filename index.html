Non-coding & regulatory variants in the RHD gene

A curated, searchable catalog of promoter, UTR, splice-site, intronic, and structural variants affecting RhD antigen expression, compiled from primary literature, ISBT allele tables, RhesusBase, and RHeference, with gnomAD population frequencies and base-editing feasibility annotations. This is a research resource and is not intended for clinical use.

About this resource

A research catalog of non-coding and regulatory blood group variants, compiled for the study of variant-guided base editing strategies for antigen modulation. Not a clinical or diagnostic tool. See methods & disclaimers.
